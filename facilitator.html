Facilitator

A circle of people, and someone to keep it safe.

Safe enough to be honest in. That is the whole job.

Men’s groups, team offsites, grief circles, festival containers. Sound often opens the space; the conversation is what people remember. My part is structure, timing and care — so that nobody has to hold the room but me.

Spaces

Four kinds of room

  • Circles

    Monthly ongoing groups and one-off gatherings.

    6–20 people

  • Offsites & away days

    Designed with you. Movement, sound, and structured conversation.

    Half or full day

  • Festivals

    Holding a tent for a weekend — schedule, crew briefing and closing ceremony included.

    A weekend

  • Community events

    Open gatherings for a neighbourhood, a group, a season turning.

    Open door
How a gathering unfolds
  1. 01Arrival

    People come in from wherever they were.

  2. 02Opening

    Why we’re here, and how we’ll be together.

  3. 03Sound

    To settle the room before anyone has to speak.

  4. 04Participation

    The circle, the conversation, the work itself.

  5. 05Reflection

    What landed, and what to take.

  6. 06Closing

    A clear end, so people can leave well.
